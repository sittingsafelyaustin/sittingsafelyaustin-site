About Sitting Safely Austin

I'm Janeth, a certified Child Passenger Safety Technician (CPST) dedicated to keeping Austin-area families safe on the road.

With hands-on experience and ongoing training, I specialize in personalized, in-person car seat installations, safety checks, and parent education. My goal is simple: help every family leave with a correctly installed seat and the confidence to know how to keep it that way.

I serve Austin and surrounding areas (based near San Antonio), offering flexible appointments at your home, driveway, or a safe location. I provide support for:

  • ✔ Newborns & rear-facing infant seats
  • ✔ Convertible and all-in-one seats
  • ✔ Booster transitions
  • ✔ Multiples, twins, and special needs setups
  • ✔ Vehicle compatibility and Texas car seat laws education

I believe every parent deserves calm, thorough guidance — not rushed group events. Let's make sure your child's ride is as safe as possible.
