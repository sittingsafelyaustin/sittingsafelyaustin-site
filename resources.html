Newborn & Infant Car Seat Tips

Key things to check: harness at or below shoulders, chest clip at armpit level, no more than 1 finger under harness.

Common mistake: Using bulky winter coats in the seat—switch to thin layers.

Booster Seat Readiness Checklist

  • Child can sit all the way back against vehicle seat
  • Knees bend naturally at edge without slouching
  • Seat belt fits low across hips & shoulder (not neck)
  • Can stay seated properly for entire ride
