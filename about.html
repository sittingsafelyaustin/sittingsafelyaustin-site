About Sitting Safely Austin

Hi, I'm Janeth — a certified Child Passenger Safety Technician (CPST) with years of hands-on experience helping Austin-area families keep their children safe on the road.

My journey started at a local fire department as an outreach coordinator. First responders were seeing too many heartbreaking crashes where children weren't properly restrained. I knew we could do better. So I became a certified CPST and launched a community initiative that provided free car seats and education to families who needed them most. That work opened my eyes to the real gaps in child passenger safety — and I never looked back.

From there, I joined the Kids in Cars program at Dell Children's Medical Center (part of Ascension Seton), where I served as a program coordinator. I performed hundreds of seat checks, became specially trained in special needs installations, led Safe Kids certification trainings for new technicians, and collaborated with TxDOT on car seat check events across Central Texas. I covered Bastrop, Caldwell, Hays, Travis, and Williamson counties — delivering hands-on help, parent education, and even sharing safety tips through PSAs on FOX 7 Austin.

Today, through Sitting Safely Austin, I bring that same dedication and expertise directly to you — personalized, no-rush appointments at your home or a convenient location. Whether you're installing a newborn rear-facing seat, transitioning to a booster, managing multiples, or navigating special needs accommodations, I'm here to make sure your child rides safely and you feel confident every time you buckle up.

Safety isn't just my job — it's my passion. Let's work together to give your family the peace of mind they deserve.
